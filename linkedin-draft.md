I built three Power BI portfolio case studies around operations analytics, using synthetic data throughout.

The demos explore warehouse service and picking productivity, inventory replenishment risk, and carrier reliability. Each includes a small data model, Power Query transformations, DAX measures, and a dashboard concept.

The goal was to practice turning operational questions into clear metrics: where service slips, which stock positions need review, and how route cost compares with punctuality.

These are fictional portfolio exercises, not results from an employer or customer.
